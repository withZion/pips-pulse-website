FAQ

The questions everyone asks me first.

Is this another signal group?

No. A signal group tells you where to buy and sell, and you're stuck trusting someone else's entry. Pips Pulse is a scanner: it finds where a setup has completed and shows you its levels. You bring the entry. That's why it works with your trading style instead of replacing it.

Why not just keep trading the pairs I already watch?

Because your strategy needs enough chances for its edge to show. Watching a handful of pairs, one or two losses can make a good week look bad. Seeing its setups across 44 markets, you pick the best of many, and a 1:2 or 1:3 risk to reward has room to work.

So no entry, stop loss or take profit?

Right, and that's deliberate. You get the range, the rejection and the breakout, everything you need to judge the setup. Where you get in is yours to decide. If you'd like a head start, the pinned videos show how I do it.

How reliable is it, honestly?

About 70% of the setups sent to members play out as expected: price pulls back into the breakout, holds it, and carries on. Every one is followed after it's sent. On top of that, picking my own entry and waiting for confirmation takes me to 70 to 80% on the setups I take. Past results don't guarantee future ones.

I'm new to this kind of setup. Will I know what to do?

Yes. Two videos are pinned in the channel: how Pips Pulse works, how to read each signal, how to use it, and a few tips from my own entry models. Watch them once and the alerts make sense from day one.

I barely have time to trade. Is it still worth it?

That's exactly who it's for. A setup is waiting at any hour, weekends included through crypto. Ten minutes is enough to look at the latest ones and pick what fits.

How many setups will I get?

It depends on the market. A quiet day brings a handful; a busy day can bring 20 or more. You never have to take them all, only the ones that fit.
